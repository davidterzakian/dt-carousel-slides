Condo or plex? 5 Laval differences to weigh 🏢

Both can bring in rent. But they're very different buys, and the right one depends on your budget, your time and how hands-on you want to be.

Price first: per Centris, Q3 2026, the median Laval plex (2–5 units) sold for $890,000, about twice the median condo at $439,999. Plexes are also rarer: 65 plex sales vs 236 condo sales that quarter, with both taking about two months to sell.

Repairs work differently too. Condo owners share big repairs through fees and a contingency fund (per CMHC). In a plex, the roof, foundation and snow removal are on you.

Renting a condo means building by-laws apply to your tenant, you notify the syndicate, and short-term rentals can be banned (per CMHC and OACIQ). In a plex, you're the landlord for every unit.

And sector matters. Per Centris (last 4 quarters to Q3 2026), median plex prices were $922,000 in Chomedey, $878,312 in Laval-des-Rapides and $802,875 in Pont-Viau. Talk to your mortgage broker and notary before you decide. I'll give it to you straight, no arrogance, no fluff.
