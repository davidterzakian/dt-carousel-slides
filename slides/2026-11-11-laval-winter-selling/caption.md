Selling in Laval this winter? Read this first. ❄️

Buyers have more to choose from right now. Per QPAREB (Centris data), October 2026, sales in Greater Montreal (Laval included) were down 18% from last October, and active listings were up almost 20%.

That's why pricing by sector matters. Per Centris.ca (last 4 quarters to Q3 2026), the single-family median was $629,750 for all of Laval, $566,125 in Fabreville and $532,250 in Pont-Viau. Your street tells a better story than the island average.

Winter adds its own details. Snow can cover a roof, a foundation or a balcony during a buyer's inspection, so keep your roof and repair invoices handy.

The seller's declarations form is required when you sell with a broker (per OACIQ). Starting it early, with your permits and warranties in hand, makes the whole process calmer. Unsure about an answer? Ask your notary.
