Before you hire a Laval home inspector, run these 5 checks.

The inspection is often the biggest unknown between an accepted offer and the keys. Per Centris (last 4 quarters, Nov 2026), the median house price is $747,812 in Sainte-Dorothée and $541,437 in Saint-François. That's a lot riding on a few hours.

Since October 2024, the RBQ (Québec's building authority) certifies pre-purchase inspectors. Per the RBQ, the certificate becomes mandatory for every inspector on October 1, 2027. You can check any inspector's name in the RBQ's free online registry.

Québec also has a standard for these inspections: BNQ 3009-500 (per the RBQ, July 2022). It's fair to ask your inspector if they follow it.

Watch the clock too. In the OACIQ's example, the buyer had 10 days to inspect, then 4 days to notify the seller in writing with the report. Your own deadlines are in your promise to purchase, so go over them with your broker and notary.
