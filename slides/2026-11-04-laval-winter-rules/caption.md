New to Laval? 5 winter rules to know first. ❄️

Your first snow season here comes with a few local rules. Nobody hands you a list when you move, so here's mine.

Winter tires are required on every Québec-registered car from December 1 to March 15, with a $200 to $300 fine if you skip them (per Québec.ca, November 2026).

Temporary car shelters can go up October 15 and must come down by April 30. Keep them 0.6 m from the curb and 1.5 m from a fire hydrant (per Ville de Laval bylaw).

When crews clear your street, one side closes, usually 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Flashing signs mean no parking, and orange temporary signs come first. Sign up for Info-Stationnement or 311 alerts. And your snow stays on your lot: Laval bans blowing or pushing it onto the street or sidewalk, contractors included (per Ville de Laval).

Then go enjoy it. Centre de la nature in Duvernay has skating, tube sliding and cross-country skiing (per Tourisme Laval). Let's break this down together. No rush.
