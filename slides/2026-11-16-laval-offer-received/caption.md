Got an offer on your Laval home? Read this first.

A promise to purchase is exciting, but it comes with dates and choices. Let's break this down together. No rush.

You can accept, refuse or send a counter-proposal. Keep an eye on the acceptance deadline: if it passes, the promise is cancelled (per OACIQ, November 2026).

Most offers come with conditions, like financing, an inspection or a document review. Each one has its own deadline, so the sale isn't final until they're met or waived.

Buyers have more to compare right now. Active single-family listings in Laval are up 17% year over year, and homes sold in 36 days on average (per Centris, Q3 2026).

The promise also sets the notary date, the occupancy date and the adjustments. The buyer pays the notary and the welcome tax. For anything specific to your file, talk to your notary.
