Pricing your Laval home? Drop these 4 myths.

Your price is the first thing buyers judge. A few common beliefs can steer it in the wrong direction, so let's clear them up.

Myth 1: your tax assessment. Laval's 2025–2027 roll reflects market conditions as of July 1, 2023 (per Ville de Laval). Buyers pay today's prices.

Myth 2: the sector median. Per Centris (last 4 quarters to Q3 2026), the median house sold for $723,750 in Duvernay and $650,968 in Sainte-Rose. A median mixes every size and age, so compare homes like yours.

Myth 3: active listings. In Q3 2026, Sainte-Rose had 100 houses for sale and 71 sales (per Centris). Asking prices are hopes. Solds are proof.

Myth 4: price high, drop it later. Duvernay houses sold in 27 days on average in Q3 2026 (per Centris). Starting near recent solds keeps you in that pace.
