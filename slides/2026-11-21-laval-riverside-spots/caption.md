Laval is an island. 5 riverside spots to know.

Moving here and want to live near the water? Laval sits between the Rivière des Prairies and the Rivière des Mille Îles, and each riverside area feels different.

Île Bigras, part of Îles-Laval, has its own REM station, about 27 minutes to Central Station at full service (per REM, November 2026).

Vieux-Saint-Vincent-de-Paul was settled in 1740, near the Rivière des Prairies, with a heritage main street and a church built in 1853 (per Bonjour Québec). Auteuil, on the Mille Îles, grew from a summer-cottage area, and Laval-sur-le-Lac sits where three waters meet, with low-density streets and a golf club since 1917 (per Ville de Laval heritage studies).

Before you fall for a view, look up the address on Québec.ca's flood-zone tool and ask your insurer about flood coverage.
