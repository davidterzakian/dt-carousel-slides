Own a Laval plex? Read your 2026 tax bill.

Whether your building is in Chomedey, Pont-Viau or Sainte-Rose, the same city rates apply. What changes the bill is your unit count and your assessed value.

Under 6 units, the 2026 general rate is $0.4973 per $100 of value. At 6 units and up, it's $0.6629, about 33% higher (per Ville de Laval 2026 budget, January 2026).

Water service is billed per dwelling: $486 each in 2026, up from $337. A triplex pays $1,458 on that line alone.

Example: a triplex valued at $900,000 would owe about $6,776 to the city in 2026 (general tax, water service, ARTM and water tax). Your real assessment sets your real bill, and Laval spreads value changes over its 2025–2027 roll. For your own situation, talk to your accountant.
