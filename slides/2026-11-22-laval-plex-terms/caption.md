Looking at a Laval plex? Learn these 5 terms.

Every plex listing comes with numbers. Before you compare buildings in Chomedey, Laval-des-Rapides or anywhere on the island, it helps to know what those numbers mean.

MRB (multiplicateur de revenu brut) is the price divided by a full year of gross rents. Same price, more rent, lower MRB. For context, the median plex sold for $922,000 in Chomedey, $878,312 in Laval-des-Rapides and $872,625 across Laval, per Centris (last 4 quarters to Q3 2026).

Operating costs come off the top: taxes, insurance, heating you pay, repairs and an allowance for empty units. Gross rents minus those costs gives you NOI, net operating income, before any mortgage.

Cap rate (TGA) is NOI divided by price, times 100. Cash flow is what's left after your mortgage payments. Your mortgage broker and accountant can help you run the real numbers on a specific building.
