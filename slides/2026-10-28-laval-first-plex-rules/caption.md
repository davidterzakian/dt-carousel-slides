Your first Laval plex: 5 rules before you offer.

A duplex or triplex isn't just a bigger house. The tenants, their leases and the rules come with it, so it pays to know them before you write an offer.

Living there changes your down payment. Per CMHC (October 2026), a live-in duplex needs 5% on the first $500K and 10% above, a live-in 3–4 unit needs 10%, and a rental-only 2–4 unit needs 20%. Your mortgage broker confirms what applies to you.

The leases come with the building, and you can't change them right after buying (per the TAL). Rent increases follow TAL rules too: 3.1% was the base rate for 2026 renewals (per TAL and Narcity, January 2026), and it's a reference, not a cap.

Looking near the water in Laval-Ouest, Îles-Laval or Saint-Vincent-de-Paul? Search the address on Québec's flood zone map first. Then run the real costs with your accountant.
