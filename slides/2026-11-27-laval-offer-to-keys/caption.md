Offer accepted in Laval? 5 steps to the keys.

The yes is a big moment, but it isn't the finish line. Whether it's a house in Fabreville (median single-family price $560,000 in Q3 2026, per Centris) or a condo near Île Paton in Chomedey, the path to moving day looks the same.

1. Clear each condition on time. Inspection, financing and documents each have their own deadline in your promise. If financing isn't confirmed in time, the seller can cancel (per OACIQ).

2. Pick your notary early. As a rule, the buyer chooses. Late documents can push back the day you take possession (per Éducaloi and DuProprio).

3. Get the money to the notary. Your down payment and loan must arrive before you sign, and bank clearing can take up to 10 days. If you finance, you'll need home insurance at least equal to the mortgage (per Éducaloi and OACIQ).

4. Plan two signings a few days apart: the mortgage, then the deed of sale. Each gets registered about two or three days later (per Éducaloi).

5. Then watch for the welcome tax bill from Ville de Laval. Pay before the 31st day after it's sent, or interest starts (per Québec.ca, Nov 2026). For loan questions, talk to your mortgage broker; for the deed, your notary.
