Selling in Laval? Read these 5 numbers first.

Before you pick a list price, it helps to know what the market did last quarter. Here's the Laval picture, per Centris.ca, Q3 2026 (July to September).

The single-family median was $630,000, up 5% from Q3 2025. Condos sat at $439,999 and plexes at $890,000.

There were 1,545 active listings (up 10%) and 848 sales (down 15%). Buyers have more choice, so price and prep carry more weight.

Houses took 36 days on average to sell, condos 57 and plexes 58. Sector matters too: Duvernay houses had a $735,000 median and sold in 27 days, Vimont $700,000, Chomedey $640,000 in 34 days.

These are city-wide numbers, not a value for your home. Your street, your house and recent solds nearby tell the real story.
