Cold Laval weekend? 5 indoor spots to know.

New to Laval, or about to be? Winter here is long, and it helps to know where to go when it's too cold for the trails.

The Cosmodôme is Canada's only museum devoted entirely to space exploration (per Société des musées du Québec). Right next door, the Musée de la santé Armand-Frappier opened in January 2023 with exhibits and hands-on labs (per Tourisme Laval).

Place Bell, in downtown Laval, offers public skating on its community rinks all year (per Place Bell). Check the schedule before you head over.

For culture, the Maison des arts de Laval on boulevard de la Concorde Ouest has been open since 1986, and Salle André-Mathieu in Laval-des-Rapides hosts theatre, music and comedy (per Bonjour Québec and Tourisme Laval).
