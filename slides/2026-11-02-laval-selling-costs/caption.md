Selling in Laval? Budget for these 5 costs.

Your sale price isn't what lands in your account. A few costs come off the top, and it's easier when you see them coming.

Commission isn't set by law in Québec. You agree on it upfront, and GST (5%) and QST (9.975%) are added on top. On a $10,000 commission, that's $1,497.50 in taxes (per OACIQ and Revenu Québec, 2026).

You also need a certificate of location that shows the property as it is today. If yours is over 10 years old, notaries usually ask for a new one (per OACIQ). Selling before your mortgage term ends can bring a prepayment penalty, plus discharge fees at closing (per FCAC and the Chambre des notaires). Your lender, notary and accountant can give you your exact numbers.

Then there's time. In Q3 2026, Laval single-family homes averaged 36 days on the market and condos 57 days (per Centris.ca). Taxes and bills keep running while you wait.
