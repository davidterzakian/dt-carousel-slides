Own a Laval plex? 5 rental rules to know.

Whether your plex is in Pont-Viau or Saint-Vincent-de-Paul, the same Québec rental rules apply. Knowing them early saves a lot of stress.

Every new residential lease must use the official TAL form. It's been mandatory since 1996 (per the Tribunal administratif du logement).

You can ask for the first month's rent in advance, but not a required security deposit. A deposit is only legal if the tenant freely offers it (per OACIQ and Éducaloi).

Raising rent on a 12-month lease? Send notice 3 to 6 months before the lease ends. The tenant has 1 month to answer, and silence counts as accepted. If they refuse, you have 1 month to apply to the TAL, or the lease renews at the same rent (per TAL form TAL-810, 2026).

For context, the median Laval plex sold for $872,625 and $802,875 in Pont-Viau over the last 4 quarters (per Centris, Q4 2025 to Q3 2026). The leases come with that price, so read every one with your notary before you offer. Let's break this down together. No rush.
