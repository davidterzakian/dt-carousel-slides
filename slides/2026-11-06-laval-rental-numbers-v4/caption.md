Buying a Laval rental? Read these 5 numbers first.

CMHC's latest rental survey (October 2025) is the clearest picture we have of Laval's rental market. Per CMHC via Courrier Laval, December 2025, vacancy rose to 3.4%, up from 2.4% a year earlier.

The average rent for occupied private units was $1,347 a month, up 8.3% in one year. Units listed for rent asked about $1,959, roughly $612 more (per CMHC via Courrier Laval, March 2026). That gap is why the leases that come with a building matter so much.

Vacancy wasn't even across price points: 0.8% for the cheapest quarter of units, 7.7% for the priciest. And in Laval-des-Rapides, average rent rose 20.7% in one year (per CMHC data via FRAPRU and Courrier Laval, December 2025).

These are past numbers, not a promise of future rents or returns. Talk to your accountant and mortgage broker before you buy.
