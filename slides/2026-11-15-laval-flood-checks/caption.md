Buying near Laval's rivers? 5 flood checks before you offer.

A view of the Rivière des Mille Îles or the Rivière des Prairies is a big reason people fall for a home in Sainte-Rose, Îles-Laval or Saint-Vincent-de-Paul. Before you write an offer, take an hour to do these checks.

1. Look up the address or lot number on Québec's interactive flood map. 2. Know that Québec's new flood-zone rules have applied since March 1, 2026, and new maps will replace ones mostly over 30 years old (per Québec.ca).

3. Remember spring 2019: per Ville de Laval (April 30, 2019), 1,234 buildings were affected by water, 200 were flooded and about 700 addresses were cut off. Ask about the flood history of any riverside street.

4. Read the seller's declarations closely. Per OACIQ guidance (April 2026), flood questions include nearby watercourses, flooded neighbours and past damage. 5. Call your insurer: per the Insurance Bureau of Canada, overland flood and sewer backup coverage are optional add-ons.
