New to Laval? 5 woods to try this winter 🌲

When you're picking a sector, look past the house. A good trail ten minutes away changes how a Laval winter feels.

Bois Papineau in Duvernay covers more than 100 hectares with 6.9 km of trails and free parking (per Bonjour Québec). Bois de l'Équerre, on rang de l'Équerre near boulevard Dagenais, has 12.95 km of trails, 1.65 km of them adapted for reduced mobility, plus snowshoe rentals (per Bonjour Québec).

In Sainte-Rose, Parc de la Rivière-des-Mille-Îles has 13 km of winter trails and free snowshoe loans, entering on rue Hotte behind the church (per the park's website). Parc Bernard-Landry in Laval-des-Rapides is a 30-hectare park with a lake, sledding, skating and cross-country skiing (per Tourisme Laval and Ville de Laval).

Out west, Bois Sainte-Dorothée has 6 km of winter trails for snowshoeing or cross-country skiing (per Tourisme Laval).

My tip: before you commit to a sector, spend a Saturday at the park closest to it. Let's break this down together. No rush.
