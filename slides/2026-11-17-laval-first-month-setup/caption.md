Just moved to Laval? Set up these 5 things.

The boxes are unpacked. Now come the small city things nobody tells you about. Let's break this down together. No rush.

First, look up your collection zone at laval.ca/recherche-par-adresse and save your 2026 calendar. Bins go out after 7 p.m. the night before (or before 7 a.m.) and come back in by noon the next day. In winter, the brown bin is often picked up every two weeks, depending on your housing type (per Ville de Laval, November 2026).

Save 311 in your phone (450-978-8000 from outside Laval) and open a Mon dossier account. That's where you request a missing bin or report a problem.

Your library card is free as a Laval resident. Bring ID and proof of residence to a branch like Sylvain-Garneau in Sainte-Rose or Philippe-Panneton in Laval-Ouest (per Bibliothèques de Laval).

Paint, electronics and tires go to the Écocentre de Vimont on Boulevard Dagenais Ouest. Old furniture? The city collects bulky items by reservation.
