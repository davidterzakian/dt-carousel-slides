Buying a Laval condo? Check these 5 things first.

When you buy a condo, you also buy into the building. Its paperwork tells you more than the showing does.

Start with price. Per Centris.ca, Q3 2026, the condo median was $450,000 in Chomedey, where condos took 59 days on average to sell. In Vimont it was $371,500 over the last 4 quarters.

Since Aug. 14, 2025, the seller must give you the syndicate's certificate on the condition of the building: fund balance, 3 years of fees, planned repairs and more. The syndicate has 15 days to issue it (per OACIQ and Québec.ca).

Then read the contingency fund study (required every 5 years), the maintenance log, the by-laws and the meeting minutes. Syndicates generally have until 2028 to get their first study and log, so ask what exists today.
