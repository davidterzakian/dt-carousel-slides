5 ways to get from Laval to Montreal, and why your sector decides which one you'll use.

If you're moving to Laval and working in Montreal, the commute is worth sorting out before you fall for a street.

Metro: the orange line stops at Cartier in Pont-Viau, plus De la Concorde and Montmorency in Laval-des-Rapides (per STL, October 2026).

REM: Sainte-Dorothée and Île-Bigras stations have linked west Laval to downtown Montreal since November 17, 2025 (per STL and Time Out Montréal).

Train: the exo2 Saint-Jérôme line stops at Sainte-Rose, Vimont and De la Concorde (per STL). Driving? Seven road bridges cross the Rivière des Prairies (per Wikipedia), so living near yours keeps the drive simpler.

My tip: do the trip at rush hour from the sector you like before you decide. Let's break this down together. No rush.
