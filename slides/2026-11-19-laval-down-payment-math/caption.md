How much down for a Laval home? Here's the math.

The minimum down payment isn't a flat 5%. It's 5% on the first $500,000, then 10% on the part above it, and 20% once the price hits $1.5 million (per the Financial Consumer Agency of Canada).

Take Fabreville's median house, $566,125 over the last 4 quarters (per Centris, Q3 2026). The minimum down works out to $31,613, about 5.6% of the price. A Fabreville condo at the $397,100 median: $19,855. An Auteuil house at $640,625: $39,063.

Under 20% down, you also pay mortgage loan insurance. With 5% to 9.99% down, CMHC's premium is 4.00% of the loan, about $21,381 on that Fabreville example. It's usually added to the mortgage, but the Québec sales tax on it isn't (per CMHC, November 2026).

Then the stress test: lenders qualify you at the greater of 5.25% or your rate plus 2% (per OSFI and nesto, 2026). Your mortgage broker can tell you exactly where you land.
