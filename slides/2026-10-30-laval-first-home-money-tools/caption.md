First Laval home? Know these 5 money tools.

Before you start saving, look at a real number. Per Centris.ca, Q3 2026, the Laval condo median was $439,999, $450,000 in Chomedey, and $374,375 in Laval-des-Rapides over the last 4 quarters.

The FHSA lets you put in up to $8,000 a year, $40,000 lifetime. Per the Canada Revenue Agency, contributions are generally deductible and qualifying withdrawals for your first home aren't taxed.

The Home Buyers' Plan lets you withdraw up to $60,000 from your RRSP and pay it back over 15 years. First withdrawals from 2026 to 2028 start repayment in the fifth year after (per CRA).

Two credits to ask about: Québec's new refundable credit, up to $1,400 from the 2026 tax year (per Revenu Québec, October 2026), and the federal Home Buyers' Amount, $10,000 on line 31270.
